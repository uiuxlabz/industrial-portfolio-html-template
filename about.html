Our Story

Building the Future, One Project at a Time

Founded in 1987 by a team of visionary engineers, INDUSTRO began as a modest structural consultancy in Houston, Texas. What started with a handful of passionate professionals has grown into a global industrial powerhouse operating across 40+ countries.

Our journey has been defined by relentless innovation and an unwavering commitment to precision. From our first major contract — a regional power facility — to今天的 complex multinational projects, we have consistently pushed the boundaries of what industrial engineering can achieve.

Today, INDUSTRO stands at the forefront of industrial innovation, combining deep domain expertise with cutting-edge technology to deliver solutions that power economies and shape infrastructure across the globe.
